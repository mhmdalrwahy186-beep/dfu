There is no source drawing for this case. The geometry in `plan.json` was invented by
the skill author to give `runtime-starter` something to render out of the box:

- interior envelope 7.82 m x 5.60 m, ceiling 2.70 m
- living 4.50 x 3.60 m, kitchen strip 4.50 x 1.88 m
- bedroom 3.20 x 3.00 m, bathroom 1.80 x 2.00 m, L-shaped hall
- balcony 2.40 x 1.20 m behind a 1.05 m parapet

not because anything was measured on site. See `plan.json` assumption `as-authored`.
