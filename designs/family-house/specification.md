# Specification: Family House 15 x 30

Concept design - not for construction. Revision A, 2026-11-13.

## Site strategy

- House on the street half of the plot: 5.5 m front garden with a driveway (second car) and a
  separate path to the front door; 12.5 m rear garden to the north.
- 1.5 m clear on both sides (at the side setbacks).
- Terrace of 7.8 x 3.0 m outside the living and dining sliders.

## Circulation

- Front door (0.9 m) to the hall. The stair rises east along the front wall: 17 risers of about
  176 mm, goings of about 263 mm, 1.0 m wide.
- The landing reaches every upstairs room; the study door is at the head of the stair.
- Garage to hall through a 0.8 m door.

## Openings and facades

- North (garden): 3.2 m and 2.8 m sliding doors to the living and dining areas, 2.2 m kitchen
  window; three 2.2-2.8 m bedroom windows above.
- South (street): front door, stair windows on both floors, a high WC window, the garage door, and the study window.
- East: window over the sink, garage side window, bathroom, study and bedroom 3 windows.
- West: living-room window, laundry window, main-bedroom window.
- Flat roof with a 0.5 m parapet. White render, dark frames.

## Open issues

1. **Sun.** The garden faces north. In the northern hemisphere, the living facade gets little
   direct sun, the street side gets the most, and summer evening sun comes from the west. Confirm
   the hemisphere. If needed, add rooflights or larger west glazing, or swap the plan to put the
   living space on the sunnier side.
2. **Garage door to the hall**: needs fire-rated, self-closing construction under most codes.
3. **Escape windows**: upstairs bedroom windows must meet local egress sizes.
4. **Laundry** is compact at 3.7 m² (2.2 x 1.7 m). If more space is needed, a utility bench can go along the garage's west wall.
5. **Structure**: the 11.4 m clear span of the living space and the 6.2 m sliders need beams or
   columns sized by an engineer. First-floor partitions do not all sit over ground-floor walls.
6. **Local rules** were not named (height, coverage, parking, energy), so the engine's defaults apply.
